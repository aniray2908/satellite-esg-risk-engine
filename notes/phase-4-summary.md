# Phase 4 — Cross-Asset Risk Scoring

## Objective

Phase 4 introduced structured risk scoring
on top of validated exposure metrics.

The goal was to move from signal extraction
to cross-asset comparability.

---

## Portfolio Coverage

Four mining assets were analyzed:

- Carajás (Brazil)
- Gevra (India)
- Bingham Canyon (USA)
- Grasberg (Indonesia)

This enabled normalization across diverse geographies and exposure levels.

---

## Deliverables

- Structured feature engineering (F1, F2, F3)
- Versioned scoring framework (CERI v1 → v2)
- Z-score normalization across portfolio
- Composite exposure score (CERI_z)
- Automated clustering and risk tiering
- Silhouette score evaluation

---

## Outcome

Phase 4 established:

- A reproducible exposure scoring pipeline
- Stable cross-asset comparison
- Automated segmentation
- Clean version progression

The system now supports adding new assets
without structural redesign.

---

## Next Steps

Future improvements may include:

- Expanded asset coverage
- Weight sensitivity refinement
- Additional satellite-derived features
- Broader portfolio benchmarking
