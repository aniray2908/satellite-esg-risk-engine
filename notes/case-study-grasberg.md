# Case Study 04 — Grasberg Mine (Indonesia)

## Objective

Validate the exposure pipeline in a high-altitude tropical mining environment
with heavy cloud presence and complex terrain.

---

## Location

- Asset: Grasberg Mine
- Country: Indonesia
- Type: Open-pit copper and gold mine
- Buffer: 1 km pit-centered radius
- Timeframe: 2019–2023
- Data: Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance (Full-Year Median Composite)

---

## Metrics Extracted

- Mean NDVI
- Low NDVI Fraction (NDVI < 0.2)
- Image count per year

---

## Observations

- Mean NDVI remained near zero (slightly negative in some years).
- Low NDVI fraction was consistently ~1.0.
- Exposure footprint remained stable across years.
- Image counts slightly lower due to regional cloud coverage but sufficient for compositing.

---

## Interpretation

The pit-centered buffer captured predominantly exposed rock and excavation zones.

Despite tropical vegetation in surrounding areas,
the core mining footprint exhibited:

- Near-total exposure
- High persistence
- Minimal vegetation recovery within the buffer

---

## Role in Portfolio

Grasberg strengthens cross-climate validation by:

- Confirming pipeline stability under cloud-heavy conditions
- Demonstrating consistency in mountainous terrain
- Reinforcing high-exposure asset behavior within CERI modeling
