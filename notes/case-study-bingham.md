# Case Study 03 — Bingham Canyon Mine (USA)

## Objective

Apply the stabilized exposure pipeline to a large-scale open-pit copper mine
to evaluate signal robustness in a mountainous environment.

---

## Location

- Asset: Bingham Canyon Mine
- Country: United States
- Type: Open-pit copper mine
- Buffer: 1 km pit-centered radius
- Timeframe: 2019–2023
- Data: Sentinel-2 Surface Reflectance (Full-Year Median Composite)

---

## Metrics Extracted

- Mean NDVI
- Low NDVI Fraction (NDVI < 0.2)
- Image count per year

---

## Observations

- Mean NDVI remained near zero across all years.
- Low NDVI fraction was consistently ~1.0.
- Minimal year-to-year variation observed.
- Exposure footprint remained stable and highly concentrated.

---

## Interpretation

The 1 km pit-centered buffer captured almost entirely exposed excavation surface.

This site represents:

- Ultra-high exposure intensity
- Extremely stable exposed footprint
- Minimal vegetation presence within core mining zone

Bingham provides a strong high-exposure anchor within the portfolio.

---

## Role in Portfolio

Bingham contributes:

- A clear upper-bound exposure case
- High persistence profile
- Stable reference for normalization in CERI v2

This case supports portfolio-level scaling of the exposure framework.
